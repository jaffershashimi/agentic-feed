D1. Arms: Arm A [BALANCE REDACTED] and Arm B [BALANCE REDACTED] of paper equity. Arm A uses the live desk entry rules. Arm B is identical except that when the chosen option's IV / 20-day realized vol is above 1.5 (for SPY or QQQ, the feed's vix_over_rv20 above 1.5), or no contract fits the size cap, it takes the stock or ETF instead.
D2. Instruments: long only. US-listed liquid stocks and ETFs, plus single-leg long options or debit spreads on SPY, QQQ and liquid large caps. Not allowed: 0DTE, shorting, futures, forex, crypto, and leverage.
D3. Score and size: a paper entry needs a conviction score of 6 or more. Size is 10% of the arm's equity at score 6, 7 or 8 and 15% at 9-10. Every entry records its score so the review can compare score 6, 7 and 8 results separately. Options will be rare at these sizes, and Arm B's stock switch will be used often.
D4. Limits per arm: at most 3 open positions; at most 5 entries a day; no first-loss stop in paper (the live desk keeps its rule); option stop 15% (rounded UP to the cent, never looser); stock stop -20%; +30% target; the fading-momentum rule; no entries before 6:45 PT or after 12:45 PT. Total open size per arm may not exceed 75% of its equity.
D5. Quotes: Robinhood real-time quotes are the reference price; quote_time is the quote's own timestamp; buys at the ask or worse, sells at the bid or worse. If a fill must use a TradingView OPRA option quote (15 minutes delayed, no timestamp): quote_time = fetch time minus 15 minutes, at most 20 minutes old at the fill, marked DELAYED_15M.
D6. Costs: options $0.68 per contract per side; stocks and ETFs $0.00, recorded as 0 (a known value). An unknown fee is left blank and makes the trade unmeasured.
D7. Versioning: policy_hash = the first 12 hex characters of sha256 of this file's text with the Status line removed. Any change creates a new version, starts a new experiment, and is recorded in claude/paper/changes.md with its evidence. Reviews only propose changes; a new version needs Jaffer's approval. Every version stays on record.
D8. Evaluation: net expectancy after fees with an interval, drawdown, tails and the comparison with Arm A, with cash and across score 6 / 7 / 8, never win rate alone. PAPER (prospective), REPLAY (historical) and SYNTHETIC results are never pooled. No fixed trade count makes a result conclusive, and nothing here changes the live desk rules.
D9. Recording: every paper and replay trade is a ledger row in claude/paper/ledger.csv.

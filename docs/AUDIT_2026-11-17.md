# System audit, 2026-11-17

Written by the orchestrator (engineer's view). Evidence is in this repo and the Agentic-Lab hub; every number has a source file or a command that reproduces it.

## Verdict in one paragraph
The system is good at telling the truth and bad at producing a result. Its research discipline, records and safety are strong. It has no validated edge, its one surviving idea (S-042) turns negative at realistic execution costs, its deterministic work runs inside a chat session that is killed whenever the container idles, and its pieces were written as separate programs rather than as one engine. The fixes are mostly engineering, and the biggest one is execution.

## Why it is not working (ranked by how much each costs)
1. **Execution cost was never modelled, and it decides the only surviving strategy.** Every backtest assumed 5 bps per side. Measured on the first 10 live S-042 names, the quoted spread was 64 bps (half 32) in the first five minutes after the open and 19 bps (half 9) at 11:00 ET. The strategy buys and sells at the open. Run through the new engine exactly as deployed: +0.255% per trade at 5 bps, **-0.285% per trade at 32 bps**, +0.228% at 10:00 ET execution (price at 10:00 vs the open: +0.5 bps for a buyer, +5.8 bps for a seller across 350 sampled trades). The first 10 paper fills averaged +40 bps against the 09:30 open, matching the model.
2. **Backtests and live tools were different programs.** Three divergences found: exits filled at the close in the backtest but at the next open live; the live stop is 90% of the signal close, the backtest used 90% of the entry open; position caps and ranking were not modelled at all (the S-044F protocol lost about 30,000 USD over ten years once they were). The pre-registered S-042 numbers (+0.98% Judge, +2.41% Final) shrink to +0.06% and +0.51% when run as implemented, before any extra cost.
3. **Deterministic work runs in the wrong runtime.** Order steps, bar archive, scans and health checks are executed inside chat sessions that cost tokens, depend on one container, and die with it: background processes were killed four times in one day. Nothing alerted anyone when the news feed lost its Benzinga source or when processes died.
4. **The idea pipeline has a low hit rate and a high cost per idea.** 49 registered ideas: 20 rejected, 7 inconclusive, 4 testing, 13 untested, 3 blocked, 1 promising, 0 adopted. Ideas were mostly crowded public technical rules tested on free data at retail speed, which is where an edge is least likely.
5. **Evidence velocity is too low.** The paper desk produced 23 closed trades in about three weeks (replay R2: 152). The go-live gate wants 100 closed trades per strategy; at that rate it is months away, and the desk's entry score does not rank outcomes (Spearman -0.03).
6. **Fragmentation.** Eleven modules each with their own loop, duplicated helpers (three Holm implementations, two bootstraps, several backtest loops), records in three places (Claude project files, Drive, GitHub), seven replay sessions unmerged since Nov 6, the project storage at 1.89 of 2.0 MB.
7. **Fragile data layer.** The 64 MB research panel lives in a temporary folder; the news source changed silently; only 40 symbols are archived; no point-in-time news.

## What is working (keep it)
| Piece | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Pre-registration and honest rejection | 27 ideas decided by rules written before the data; S-042, S-043, S-044, S-046 results reproduce from the repo |
| Safety rails | paper-host guard, kill switch, caps, broker-side stops on every entry (health check: 9 of 9 positions covered), 17 failure scenarios safe |
| Records integrity | hash-chained store, checkpoint OK, privacy check on every change, no account data in GitHub |
| Paper plumbing end to end | signal after the close, order queue, fill at the open, stop live, one stop-out recorded (SMMT, 9.7% loss as designed) |
| Statistical toolkit | PSR, Deflated Sharpe, PBO, bootstrap, tear sheets (S-042 DSR 0.36 at 42 ideas tried) |
| CI | 469 tests, privacy and registry checks on every push |
| The one live finding about reality | first-fill data now exists and is being compared to the model |

## What the fixes are
See [ENGINE.md](ENGINE.md) for the architecture and the phased plan. Built today: the engine core with a single code path for backtest and live, as-implemented backtests with execution scenarios, health checks for the failures that really happened, and a read-only preflight. Needs the owner: a deterministic runtime outside the chat session (GitHub Actions in the private hub, which needs two repository secrets), approval of an execution change for S-042 (opening-auction or 10:00 ET orders), and a decision on paid data.
